🚂 Transport Tycoon: Passenger

Build a passenger transport empire with trains, buses, trams, ships, aircraft, and metro.

  1. Build tracks/roads/canals/air routes (2-5, 0 for metro).
  2. Place stations on them (6).
  3. Draw a line (8) connecting stations.
  4. Buy vehicles for that line (Shop).
  5. Watch your cities grow and unlock new tech!
